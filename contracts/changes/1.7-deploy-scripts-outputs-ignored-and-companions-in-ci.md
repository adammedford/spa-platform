- **Raised by:** workstream 1, AWS and IaC, during #58 (task 1.7)
- **Tasks:** 1.7; I-5, whose workflow holds every test companion's steps
- **Date:** 2026-10-20
- **Status:** open

## What changed

#58 adds `tools/scripts/seed.sh`, `deploy.sh`, `rollback.sh` and `teardown.sh`, the shared
`tools/scripts/cell-common.sh` they source, and `tools/scripts/cell_documents.py`, which builds and
reads the JSON they need. Their test companions are `tools/scripts/seed_tests.py`,
`deploy_tests.py`, `rollback_tests.py` and `teardown_tests.py`, in the shape D-3 in
`docs/dependencies.md` decided: Python's `unittest`, running the real scripts under `/bin/bash`
with doubles on a constructed PATH. The four share their doubles, `tools/scripts/cell_doubles.py`,
which each loads by path and which is not a companion itself.

The companions need no AWS CLI, Terraform, Docker, .NET SDK, credentials or network: doubles stand
in for `aws`, `terraform`, `docker` and `dotnet`. They need `python3` (3.9-compatible), `/bin/bash`,
and `awk`, `sed`, `tr`, `cat`, `mktemp`, `rm`, `dirname`, `basename`, `sleep` and `date` on
the runner, which both CI images carry. Each honours `SCRIPT_TESTS_REQUIRE_BASH` as the other
companions do, and runs its cases on `tools/companion_runner.py`. On this Mac, under bash 3.2.57,
they run 12, 22, 9 and 17 tests in about 4, 9, 3 and 5 seconds.

`seed.sh` and `deploy.sh` write `infra/envs/poc/outputs.json`, the document
`contracts/infra-outputs.schema.json` says workstream 1 writes after every apply. It carries the
account id: the bucket's name, the two repositories' URIs and the gateway image all contain it.
Nothing ignores the file today (`git check-ignore infra/envs/poc/outputs.json` prints nothing).

   - in the Documents job, `python3 -I tools/scripts/<name>_tests.py`, under the runner's bash 5;
   - in the Scripts under bash 3.2 job, the same command with `SCRIPT_TESTS_REQUIRE_BASH: "3.2"`.
3. **Two sentences in `docs/dependencies.md`.**
   - D-3, in the shape of its others: "`tools/scripts/seed.sh`, `deploy.sh`, `rollback.sh` and
     `teardown.sh` have `tools/scripts/seed_tests.py`, `deploy_tests.py`, `rollback_tests.py` and
