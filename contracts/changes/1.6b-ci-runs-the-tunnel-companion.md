# Change request 1.6b: CI runs the test companion of `tunnel.sh` and `verify-tunnel.sh`

- **Raised by:** workstream 1, AWS and IaC, during #57 (task 1.6b)
- **Tasks:** 1.6b; I-5, whose workflow holds every test companion's steps
- **Date:** 2026-10-20
- **Status:** open

## What changed

#57 adds `tools/scripts/tunnel.sh`, the Session Manager port forward through the gateway task to the
internal ALB, and `tools/scripts/verify-tunnel.sh`, the check that a health path answers 200 over it
with TLS the workstation trusts. Their test companion is `tools/scripts/tunnel_tests.py`, in the
shape D-3 in `docs/dependencies.md` decided for a shell script's tests: Python's `unittest`, running
the real scripts under `/bin/bash` with doubles on a constructed PATH.

The companion needs no AWS CLI, no Session Manager plugin, no credentials and no network: its doubles
stand in for `aws`, `session-manager-plugin` and `curl`. Two of its cases run the real `curl`, one
against a loopback port nothing listens on and one against a loopback TLS listener whose
certificate, made by `openssl` for the case, no trust store holds. It needs `python3`
(3.9-compatible), `/bin/bash`, `curl`, `openssl`, and `awk`, `sed`, `tr`, `cat`, `mktemp` and `rm` on
the runner, which both CI images carry. It honours `SCRIPT_TESTS_REQUIRE_BASH` as the other
companions do. On this Mac it runs 37 tests in about 12 seconds under bash 3.2.

`.github/shell-parse.py` already reads both scripts in both jobs, because it reads every tracked
`.sh`. Nothing runs the companion.

## What is asked of the integrator

Two edits in files AGENTS.md reserves to the integrator, the same two change request issue-122 asks
for its companion.

1. **Two steps in `.github/workflows/ci.yml`**, in the shape of `tools/preflight_tests.py`'s pair:
   - in the Documents job, `python3 -I tools/scripts/tunnel_tests.py`, under the runner's bash 5;
   - in the Scripts under bash 3.2 job, the same command with `SCRIPT_TESTS_REQUIRE_BASH: "3.2"`.

   The Documents job is the place because it runs on every change: the scripts are under
   `tools/scripts/`, which neither half in `.github/touches.sh` covers.
2. **D-3 in `docs/dependencies.md`**, one sentence in the shape of its others:
   "`tools/scripts/tunnel.sh` and `tools/scripts/verify-tunnel.sh` have `tools/scripts/tunnel_tests.py`
   (#57), in both jobs: doubles for aws, session-manager-plugin and curl, and the real curl against
   two loopback listeners, so no case reaches AWS."

## What #57 did without it

The scripts and the companion, all of it. The companion passes by hand,
`python3 -I tools/scripts/tunnel_tests.py`, and each of its guards was broken on purpose to watch it
fail; the mutations and their counts are in the task's Answer on #57.

## Why this is a change request and not an edit

`.github/` and `docs/dependencies.md` are the integrator's (AGENTS.md, OWNERSHIP.md).

## Blocking

Not the sitting: Adam runs the two scripts against the applied cell (B1) whether or not CI runs the
companion. Until this lands the companion runs only by hand, so a later edit to either script can
break a case with nothing to say so, and the row cannot be done in AGENTS.md's sense, which asks for
its tests to pass in CI and no change request open.
