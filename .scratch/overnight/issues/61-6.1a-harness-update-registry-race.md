# 61: 6.1a — `UpdateRegistryAsync` reports `NotModified` when the poller's first tick lands first
**Task:** 6.1a
**Owner:** ws6-verification
**Blocked by:** None
**Status:** ready-for-agent

**What to build:** Make `GatewayHarness.UpdateRegistryAsync` and `PublishRegistryAsync`
(`tests/Gateway.TestHarness/GatewayHarness.cs`) report the outcome of the write they made, not of the
one poll they ran. On CI run https://github.com/adammedford/spa-platform/actions/runs/38849080050
(`main` at `55c4599`, a docs-only commit) two rows failed with `Expected: Swapped / Actual:
NotModified`: `UpstreamResponseTests.StartWith` (`Negative/UpstreamResponseTests.cs:136`) and
`UnknownApiTests.An_api_name_another_app_lists_is_still_unlisted_for_this_one`
(`Negative/UnknownApiTests.cs:106`). The re-run of the same commit passed, and the suite passes
locally (90 of 90, Debug, arm64). Task 6.1 is landed; this is a carve of its harness, named `6.1a` as
`6.2a` and `6.3a` were.

**Why, as the integrator reads it (confirm by reproduction before changing anything):** the hosted
poller's first tick is not ordered against the start-up settle. `GatewayHarness.Create` writes
revision 1, starts the server, then polls once "to wait out the first read" (the comment at
`GatewayHarness.cs:230`). If the poller's first tick has not begun by then, the explicit poll *is* the
first read and the tick runs later. On a slow runner it lands between a test's write and that test's
own poll: the tick swaps revision 2 in the background, the explicit poll's conditional GET then
answers 304, and `RegistrySource.RefreshOnceAsync` reports `NotModified`
(`src/Platform.Registry/RegistrySource.cs:116`) for a document that is live. The source runs one poll
at a time (`RefreshAsync` takes `polling`), so the two cannot interleave inside a poll; they
interleave around it.

**Done when:** a test in `tests/Gateway.IntegrationTests/Harness/`, beside `HarnessBootTests`,
reproduces the race deterministically (hold the write until a background refresh is forced first, or
drive `RefreshRegistryAsync` concurrently with the write) and is watched failing on the current
harness; then the harness passes it by reporting `Swapped` whenever the live snapshot's revision, or
ETag, equals the one just written, and `NotModified` only when it does not. `Failed`, `Rejected` and
`Missing` are unchanged. The two CI rows need no edit. Owner paths only: `tests/Gateway.TestHarness`
and `tests/Gateway.IntegrationTests`. Nothing in `src/` moves: the source's semantics are right, and
the harness's reading of them is what is wrong. If the reproduction shows a different mechanism,
record it under `## Answer` and fix that instead.
