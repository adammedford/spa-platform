now: it is what turns the gate on until ticket 26 lands, and ticket 36 removes it after.

**Done when:** every test project is green with `SessionRequired` defaulting to true; no composed
test sets it to false; a mutation that turns the shell gate off fails at least one row in each of
the three projects (watched failing).
